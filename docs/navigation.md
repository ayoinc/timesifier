# Navigation

The structure every signed-in page belongs to. The owner adopted it on 2026-11-11 and asked the same day for it to be checked against Harvest, the product Timesifier follows; this version is the result, widened the same day to Harvest's business features (`docs/research/harvest-b2b.md`). Since TS-064 every destination is in the product, and one whose card is not Done shows its heading and an empty state that says so. Each entry names the card that builds it.

Every Harvest fact below is cited by its address; the addresses are listed under "Sources", with the tool that read each one.

What the owner set, and what this check did with it:

- **Role counts.** Each role has one main link fewer, 3, 5 and 7 instead of 4, 6 and 8, because Dashboard is gone; the reason is in the first row above. Time off (TS-061) has since added one to each, so they are 4, 6 and 8 again. Who sees which link is otherwise as the owner set it, and the four utility items are unchanged.
- **Sign out is an action, not a page.** Harvest opens the person's profile and notification settings from their name at the top right ([members], [member-approval]); none of the sources gives signing out a page of its own, so nothing argues for changing it.
- **Clients is optional.** Harvest lets an administrator hide whole sections for everyone, "Expense tracking", "Timesheet approval", "Invoices", "Estimates", "Client dashboard" and more, and hiding one deletes no data ([modules]). Clients itself is not among them; in Harvest "all time is tracked to a task that's assigned to a project for a client" ([quick]). Timesifier's projects need no client (TS-011b), so the same kind of switch fits Clients here. TS-048 now builds it.

## Roles and counts

Counts are per role. Individual records, forms and tabs sit inside a section. The timer control is an action, not a link, and is not counted. Sign out is an action, not a page; it is one of the four utility items, as before.

| Member (employee, contractor) |          5 |             4 |       9 |
| Manager                       |          9 |             4 |      13 |
| Finance                       |         10 |             4 |      14 |
| Owner or admin                |         15 |             4 |      19 |

Time off (TS-061) is counted in every row. A manager is a member of any role who manages a team; a Finance person who manages a team sees Team and Approvals as well.

Roles are those of `docs/security.md`, "Organisations and roles": owner, admin, Finance, member, and the manager role of TS-038. Owner, admin, manager and member map onto Harvest's three classic levels ([invite], "write administrator, manager, or member"): Harvest's Administrator is our owner or admin, its Manager our manager, its Member our member. Finance is Harvest's Accounting profile reduced to one built-in role (TS-064): it sees Bill, every rate, all time and expenses read-only, every report, Dashboards and the Activity log, and administers no people. Whether a person is an employee or a contractor is a flag on their membership, not a role. Harvest's other built-in and custom roles ([permissions]) are under "Deliberately not copied".

## The navigation under 600px

Below 600px the rail becomes a bar at the foot of the screen, 64px tall (TS-064; audit findings W03 and W11).

- **The bar** holds Timesheet, Time off, Expenses, Projects and More (`compactNavigation` in `app/shell/destinations.ts`). Each item takes an equal share of the width and the bar's full height to press, at least 48px, with its label in label-medium; the labels stand at least 8px apart. A member, shown Timesheet, Time off, Expenses, Projects and Reports, has all five in the bar and no More. Workspace settings is always in More for owners and admins, so its short bar label, Settings, is kept in code but is not shown in practice.
- **More** opens a native HTML popover, which works without script, holding every other destination the person is shown, under their headings. If only one destination would go into More, it sits in the bar instead, so a member sees no More. More is marked current when the page shown is one of its destinations, and carries the number pending on Approvals.
- **"Track time"** is the timer control's one name everywhere. Under 600px it is a floating button, 56px tall, at the bottom right, 16px above the bar, not a full-width row. While a timer runs it becomes a single 56px strip straight above the bar, so the fixed chrome is the 64px bar, or the bar and that one row. It is not drawn on Timesheet under 600px, which has its own Track time and running timer.
- **Tests**: `test/pages/destinations.test.ts` (what each role's bar and More hold) and `e2e/shell-navigation.spec.ts` and `e2e/timesheet.spec.ts` at 390px (sizes, spacing, More by mouse, keyboard and without script, the fixed rows, the timer's control).

From 600px the rail shows every destination under its heading, with "Track time" at the top. An owner's fifteen links are taller than a short window (1280×800): the rail then scrolls on its own, and the page does not move with it.

## Where a person lands

Everyone lands on Timesheet, on the Day view of today, with the timer ready. That is where Harvest sends a new account first ("Head to Time > Timesheet", [quick]) and what it calls "your home for time tracking" ([nav]). Managers and admins track time too; what waits for them shows as the number on Approvals and in Notifications. Today, the Today destination of TS-012a is that page; TS-046 renames it Timesheet.

## Flows

Each flow is first Harvest's, step by step with its source, then Timesifier's through the structure above, with the card that builds each step. "Built" means it is in production now.

### 1. First sign-in and setup

Harvest:

1. A new account starts from a form asking first name, last name, company name, work email and password, for a free trial with no credit card ([signup]); the home page says the trial lasts 30 days ([home]).
2. Sign-in is at id.getharvest.com with email and password, Google, or SAML; a person in several accounts picks one after signing in ([signin]).
3. The quick start guide runs: start tracking time in Time > Timesheet, invite a teammate from Team, create a project in Projects, add tasks in Manage > Tasks, and set account preferences such as timer mode and time display in Settings > Preferences ([quick]).
4. An invited person accepts an emailed invitation, then signs in at id.getharvest.com ([members]).

- **Typing a week's hours in a grid** is TS-052, after TS-012b. An organisation of one person has nobody else to approve its week.
