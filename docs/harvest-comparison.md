# Timesifier and Harvest, page by page

The owner named Harvest as the product to follow (2026-11-11). `docs/navigation.md` was checked against Harvest's public site and help centre. This document checks the built pages against Harvest's own application, signed in to the owner's trial account, and names what Timesifier takes from it. Cards TS-056 to TS-059 build what is taken, and TS-061 Harvest's time off.

## How it was observed

rea's `capture_browser_scenario` drove a local Chrome over the DevTools protocol, carrying the owner's Harvest session copied from the cmux browser, on 2026-11-11. It only opened pages and the "Track time" dialog; nothing was saved in Harvest. The accessibility tree of each page is in `docs/research/harvest-app/`, with the address it was taken at; names, addresses and the account's name are replaced by placeholders.

## What Timesifier does not take, and why

- **Bill (invoices, estimates), expenses, tasks, budgets, rates, profitability, capacity and utilisation**: out of release 1 (`docs/navigation.md`, "Later").
- **Harvest AI**, **sample data** ("Example Client", "Example Project") and the **trial banner**: Timesifier makes nothing up in a person's account, and has no paid plan.
- **Customize sidebar**: Later, as in `docs/navigation.md`. (Its **Calendar view** is taken: TS-062.)
- **The invitee's name, employee or contractor, roles and departments on the invitation**: a person gives their own name on My account (TS-008); roles beyond owner, admin, member and manager are "Deliberately not copied" in `docs/navigation.md`.
- **A manual lock from Approvals**: approving a period locks it already (TS-046, TS-047).
